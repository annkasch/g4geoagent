# Inverted-coaxial germanium detector P43511A

## Overview

P43511A is a p-type high-purity germanium (HPGe) detector of the inverted-coaxial
point-contact type. It is a single crystal with rotational symmetry about its axis.
The detector is operated bare, immersed in liquid argon.

The crystal is a cylinder with a diameter of 71.8 mm and a height of 99.3 mm. One
flat face carries the point contact and is called the bottom face. The opposite
flat face, the top face, has a deep borehole.

## Outer shape

The outer edge of the top face and the outer edge of the bottom face are both
rounded with a radius of 2.0 mm, all the way around. The rounding is a quarter
circle in cross-section: it meets the flat face at a radius of 33.9 mm and meets
the cylindrical side surface 2.0 mm from the face.

## Borehole

The borehole is centred on the crystal axis, opens at the top face and has a
total depth of 75.0 mm, so 24.3 mm of germanium remain between the deepest point
of the borehole and the bottom face. Going from the top face downwards it has
three parts:

1. A tapered part, 50.0 mm long. Its diameter is 27.6 mm at the top face and
   decreases linearly to 10.0 mm.
2. A straight cylindrical part, 20.0 mm long, with a diameter of 10.0 mm.
3. A hemispherical end with a radius of 5.0 mm.

## Bottom face

At the centre of the bottom face there is a hemispherical dimple with a radius
of 1.0 mm, cut into the crystal. Its surface is the p+ point contact.

A ring-shaped groove surrounds the point contact. It is centred on the axis, has
an inner radius of 13.0 mm and an outer radius of 16.0 mm, a rectangular
cross-section, and is 2.0 mm deep, measured from the bottom face.

## Electrical properties and operation

The n+ contact covers the side surface, the top face, the borehole surface and
the bottom face outside the groove. The detector is biased with 3500 V on the n+
contact while the point contact is held at 0 V. The operating temperature is
77 K. The net impurity concentration is 1.8e10 per cm3 at the bottom face and
falls linearly to 1.1e10 per cm3 at the top face.

## What to build

Build the germanium crystal and its surroundings:

- The crystal is germanium enriched in Ge-76: 91% of the atoms are Ge-76 and
  the remaining 9% are Ge-74. Its density is 5.551 g/cm3. The contacts are
  surface layers and are not separate volumes.
- The world is a cube of liquid argon with an edge length of 300 mm, centred on
  the origin. The borehole, the groove and the dimple are filled with liquid
  argon.
- The crystal axis is the z axis. The centre of the bottom face is at the origin
  and the top face is at z = +99.3 mm.
