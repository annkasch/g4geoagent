# Broad-energy germanium (BEGe) detector

## Overview

This is a p-type high-purity germanium (HPGe) detector of the broad-energy
(BEGe) type. It is a single crystal with rotational symmetry about its axis.
The detector is operated bare, immersed in liquid argon.

The crystal is a short cylinder with a diameter of 71.8 mm and a height of
40.0 mm. One flat face carries the point contact and is called the bottom face.
The opposite flat face is the top face. The crystal has no borehole.

## Outer shape

The outer edge of the top face and the outer edge of the bottom face are both
rounded with a radius of 2.0 mm, all the way around. The rounding is a quarter
circle in cross-section: it meets the flat face at a radius of 33.9 mm and meets
the cylindrical side surface 2.0 mm from the face.

## Bottom face

The p+ point contact is a flat disc with a radius of 7.0 mm at the centre of
the bottom face. It is a surface layer and does not change the shape of the
crystal.

A ring-shaped groove surrounds the point contact. It is centred on the axis, has
an inner radius of 7.0 mm and an outer radius of 10.0 mm, a rectangular
cross-section, and is 2.0 mm deep, measured from the bottom face.

## Electrical properties and operation

The n+ contact covers the side surface, the top face and the bottom face outside
the groove. It is a layer about 0.4 mm thick inside the germanium; the p+ contact
layer is about 0.2 mm thick. The detector is biased with 4000 V on the n+ contact
while the point contact is held at 0 V. The operating temperature is 77 K. The
net impurity concentration is 0.88e10 per cm3 at the bottom face and falls
linearly to 0.60e10 per cm3 at the top face.

## What to build

Build the germanium crystal and its surroundings:

- The crystal is germanium enriched in Ge-76: 91% of the atoms are Ge-76 and
  the remaining 9% are Ge-74. Its density is 5.551 g/cm3. The contacts are
  layers of the same germanium and are not separate volumes.
- The world is a cube of liquid argon with an edge length of 300 mm, centred on
  the origin. The groove is filled with liquid argon.
- The crystal axis is the z axis. The centre of the bottom face is at the origin
  and the top face is at z = +40.0 mm.
