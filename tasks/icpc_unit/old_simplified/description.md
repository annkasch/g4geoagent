# Detector unit with the inverted-coaxial detector P43511A

## Overview

In the experiment each germanium detector is mounted in a detector unit. The
crystal rests on a baseplate made of the plastic PEN (polyethylene naphthalate).
Three vertical copper rods run past the crystal and carry the baseplate. The
front-end electronics for the signal readout sit on a small fused-silica
substrate, called the LMFE, which is held in a plastic frame under the
baseplate. Four thin coaxial cables carry the signal from the LMFE upwards to
the next amplifier stage, 30 cm above the detectors. A flat cable brings the
high voltage to the detector. The whole unit is immersed in liquid argon.

This document describes a simplified unit with eight kinds of parts: the
crystal, the baseplate, three supports, three rods, the LMFE frame, the LMFE
substrate, four signal cables and the high-voltage cable.

The unit is described in a coordinate system whose z axis is the crystal axis,
pointing upwards. The origin is at the centre of the bottom face of the crystal.
Angles around the z axis are measured from the +x axis towards the +y axis.

## Crystal

P43511A is a p-type high-purity germanium detector of the inverted-coaxial
point-contact type. It is a single crystal with rotational symmetry about its
axis, a cylinder with a diameter of 71.8 mm and a height of 99.3 mm. The bottom
face carries the point contact and faces the baseplate. The top face has a deep
borehole.

The outer edge of the top face and the outer edge of the bottom face are both
rounded with a radius of 2.0 mm, all the way around. The rounding is a quarter
circle in cross-section: it meets the flat face at a radius of 33.9 mm and meets
the cylindrical side surface 2.0 mm from the face.

The borehole is centred on the crystal axis, opens at the top face and has a
total depth of 75.0 mm. Going from the top face downwards it has three parts:

1. A tapered part, 50.0 mm long. Its diameter is 27.6 mm at the top face and
   decreases linearly to 10.0 mm.
2. A straight cylindrical part, 20.0 mm long, with a diameter of 10.0 mm.
3. A hemispherical end with a radius of 5.0 mm.

At the centre of the bottom face there is a hemispherical dimple with a radius
of 1.0 mm, cut into the crystal. A ring-shaped groove surrounds it. The groove
is centred on the axis, has an inner radius of 13.0 mm and an outer radius of
16.0 mm, a rectangular cross-section, and is 2.0 mm deep, measured from the
bottom face.

The crystal is germanium enriched in Ge-76: 91% of the atoms are Ge-76 and the
remaining 9% are Ge-74. Its density is 5.551 g/cm3. The detector is operated at
a bias voltage of 3500 V.

## Baseplate

The baseplate is a flat PEN plate, 1.5 mm thick, perpendicular to the crystal
axis. Its top face is 2.0 mm below the bottom face of the crystal.

Seen along the axis, the plate is a triangle with rounded corners. Its outline
is obtained from a disc with a diameter of 92.0 mm, centred on the crystal axis,
by cutting it off along three straight edges. Each straight edge is 30.0 mm
from the crystal axis at its closest point. The perpendiculars from the axis to
the three edges point in the directions 60, 180 and 300 degrees. What remains
of the circle are three short arcs, the rounded corners, which point in the
directions 0, 120 and 240 degrees.

A round hole with a diameter of 16.0 mm at the centre of the plate gives access
to the point contact.

PEN has the chemical formula C14H10O4 and a density of 1.35 g/cm3.

## Supports

The crystal does not touch the baseplate directly. It rests on three PEN
supports that stand on the top face of the baseplate and fill the 2.0 mm between
plate and crystal. Each support is a cylinder with a diameter of 6.0 mm and a
height of 2.0 mm, with its axis parallel to the crystal axis. The centres of the
supports are 25.0 mm from the crystal axis, at angles of 60, 180 and 300 degrees.

## Copper rods

Three copper rods run parallel to the crystal axis. Each rod is a solid cylinder
with a diameter of 3.0 mm and a length of 130.0 mm. The rod axes are 41.0 mm
from the crystal axis, at angles of 0, 120 and 240 degrees. Each rod starts
12.0 mm below the bottom face of the crystal and ends 18.7 mm above its top
face.

The rods pass through the three corners of the baseplate. For each rod the plate
has a round hole with a diameter of 3.2 mm, centred on the rod axis.

The copper has a density of 8.96 g/cm3.

## LMFE

The LMFE frame is a rectangular block of the plastic Ultem (polyetherimide,
chemical formula C37H24O6N2, density 1.27 g/cm3), 11.0 mm wide, 20.0 mm long
and 1.5 mm thick. It lies flat against the bottom face of the baseplate. Its
long side is parallel to the y axis and its short side parallel to the x axis.
Its centre is at x = 0 and y = -18.0 mm.

The LMFE substrate is a rectangular plate of fused silica (SiO2, density
2.20 g/cm3), 8.0 mm wide, 16.0 mm long and 0.5 mm thick. It is completely
enclosed by the frame and sits exactly at its centre, with its sides parallel
to those of the frame.

## Signal cables

Four identical coaxial cables leave the LMFE. Each cable has a diameter of
0.4 mm. A cable is not resolved into conductor and insulation: it is a solid
cylinder of one mixed material, which is half copper and half FEP (a
fluoropolymer with one carbon atom per two fluorine atoms) by mass and has a
density of 3.0 g/cm3.

The cables run side by side at x = -0.9, -0.3, 0.3 and 0.9 mm. Each cable is
modelled as two straight pieces:

1. An outward piece, parallel to the y axis and at the height of the centre of
   the LMFE frame. It starts at the end face of the frame that is furthest from
   the crystal axis and is 9.8 mm long, so it ends at y = -37.8 mm.
2. An upward piece, parallel to the z axis, with its axis at y = -38.0 mm. Its
   lower end is level with the lowest point of the outward piece. Its upper end
   is at the same height as the upper ends of the copper rods.

The two pieces of a cable touch but do not overlap.

## High-voltage cable

The high-voltage cable is a flat strip of Kapton (polyimide, density
1.42 g/cm3), 3.0 mm wide and 0.125 mm thick. It comes down past the crystal on
the side opposite the signal cables and then runs under the baseplate towards
the crystal axis. Its width is along the x axis everywhere, centred on x = 0.
It is modelled as two straight pieces:

1. A piece under the baseplate, lying flat against the bottom face of the
   plate. It runs along the y axis from y = +20.0 mm outwards and is
   17.4375 mm long, so it ends where the upward piece begins.
2. An upward piece, parallel to the z axis, with its thickness along the y
   axis and its centre at y = +37.5 mm. Its lower end is level with the bottom
   face of the first piece. Its upper end is at the same height as the upper
   ends of the copper rods.

The two pieces touch but do not overlap.

## What to build

Build the unit in a world that is a cube of liquid argon with an edge length of
300 mm, centred on the origin. Every space not filled by one of the parts above,
including the borehole, the groove, the dimple and the holes in the baseplate,
is liquid argon. Electrical contacts, bond wires and fasteners are not part of this
model.
